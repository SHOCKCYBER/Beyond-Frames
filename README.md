## 🚀 NEW: Built-in Studio Manager (Upload Directly in Your Browser)

You do **NOT** have to edit any code to customize your website!
Look at the **bottom-left corner** of your website screen:
1. Click the **`Studio Manager`** floating gold button.
2. In the menu that pops up:
   - **Tab 1: Brand Logo**: Click to choose your logo image (PNG, SVG, JPG). It instantly replaces the logos across your navbar and footer, and saves permanently in your browser!
   - **Tab 2: + Add Portfolio Work**: Choose any photo from your computer, type the title, select category (*Photography, Cinematography, Commercial, Post & Grading*), enter your camera gear, and click **"Add to Live Portfolio"**. It will appear right at the top of your portfolio grid with full lightbox integration!
   - **Tab 3: Contact & WhatsApp**: Enter your real WhatsApp number, email, and phone. All buttons on the website will instantly point to your real contact channels!
   - **Tab 4: Manage Uploads**: View or delete your uploaded projects anytime.
   - **Tab 5: Client Inquiries (Mini-CRM)**: All client booking inquiries submitted through the website are backed up here with timestamps, client phone numbers, budgets, and direct "WhatsApp Client" / "Email Client" reply buttons!
